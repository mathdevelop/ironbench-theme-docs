Release notes

To find your version, go to Online Store > Themes in your admin. The version number appears under the theme name.

Version 1.0.0

First release

The first release of Anvil, a theme for hardware, tool, and industrial supply stores.

Shopping

  • Search by product title, type, vendor, tag, variant title, SKU, or barcode, with search suggestions as customers type
  • Collection and search pages with filters, sorting, and a grid or list layout that customers can switch between
  • List layout with SKU, stock status, quantity field, and up to 3 specification metafields per product
  • Mega menu with promotion blocks, and a mobile menu drawer

Product page

  • Variant picker with buttons or dropdowns, option swatches, and updates without reloading the page
  • Specifications table from product metafields, and downloads from file metafields
  • Bulk order table for products with many variants
  • Volume pricing and quantity rules from Shopify B2B catalogs
  • Real stock status, pickup availability, unit prices, selling plans, and Shop Pay Installments messaging
  • Gift cards with a recipient form, and a printable gift card page with a QR code
  • Complementary products and related products
  • Image, video, and 3D model gallery

Cart

  • Cart drawer or cart page, with quantity changes without reloading the page
  • Order notes, discount details, and accelerated checkout buttons
  • Product suggestions in an empty cart

Design

  • Three presets: Anvil, Foundry, and Lathe
  • Five color schemes per preset, meeting WCAG AA contrast by default
  • Home page sections: Hero, Service highlights, Collection list, Featured collection, Shop by project, Multicolumn, Image with text, Logo list, Rich text, Email signup, Custom Liquid, and Custom section
  • App blocks in the product information section and the custom section

Anvil is a Shopify theme by Matheus Alves. Need help? Contact support.
