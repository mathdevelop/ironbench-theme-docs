Cart and checkout

Cart type

Choose how the cart opens in Theme settings > Cart > Cart type:

  • Drawer: the cart slides in from the side when a product is added, so customers can keep shopping.
  • Page: customers stay on the product page and see a short confirmation. The cart icon opens the full cart page.

In both cases, customers can change quantities and remove items without reloading the page. Quantity rules from B2B catalogs apply in the cart too.

Order notes

Turn on Show order notes to let customers add instructions, such as a purchase order number or delivery details. Change the field's label in Order notes label. Notes appear on the order in your admin.

Empty cart

When the cart is empty, Anvil shows a link back to the store. To suggest products, choose a collection in Collection for empty cart.

Checkout

Checkout is managed by Shopify, so it isn't styled by the theme. To change its colors and logo, go to Settings > Checkout and click Customize.

Accelerated checkout buttons, such as Shop Pay, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay, appear on the cart page and on product pages. Which buttons appear depends on your payment settings and the customer's device.

Discount codes are entered at checkout. Automatic discounts and discount codes already applied show in the cart.
