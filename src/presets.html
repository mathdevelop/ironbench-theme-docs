Presets

A preset is a starting style: colors, fonts, and corner radius. Every preset has the same sections and features, so you can switch styles at any time.

Ironbench

Safety yellow on graphite and white. Built for general hardware and industrial supply stores with large catalogs. Headings use Barlow Condensed, body text uses IBM Plex Sans.

Foundry

A dark style with copper highlights and square corners. Made for professional tools, welding, and metalworking brands that want a premium look.

Lathe

Warm paper tones with a deep green and softer corners. Suits woodworking, hand tool, and garden stores with smaller, curated catalogs. Headings use Archivo, body text uses Work Sans.

Switching presets

To try a different preset, install it from the Theme Store listing as a new theme. To keep your current content and only change the style, edit Theme settings > Colors and Typography instead.

Color schemes

Each preset includes five color schemes. Every section has a Color scheme setting, so you can alternate backgrounds down the page. Edit a scheme in Theme settings > Colors and the change applies to every section that uses it. The default schemes meet WCAG AA contrast for text and buttons. If you change them, keep text readable against its background.
