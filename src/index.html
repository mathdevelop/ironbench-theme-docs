Ironbench

Ironbench is a Shopify theme for hardware, tool, and industrial supply stores. It helps customers find the right part by SKU, compare specifications, and order in volume with as few clicks as possible.

What's included

  • Search first. The header search and the home page hero search match product titles, types, vendors, tags, variant titles, SKUs, and barcodes, with suggestions as customers type.
  • Technical list layout. Collection and search pages switch between a photo grid and a dense list that shows SKUs, stock, and up to 3 specifications per product. The choice is remembered on the customer's device.
  • Specification tables. Turn product metafields into a clean specification table, and attach manuals and data sheets as downloads.
  • Bulk order table. Customers can add several sizes or finishes of the same product to the cart at once.
  • B2B ready. Quantity rules and volume price breaks from Shopify B2B catalogs show up on the product page automatically.
  • Real stock levels. Stock status uses your actual inventory counts. Ironbench never shows fake urgency.
  • Built-in filtering. Filters and sorting come from Shopify's Search & Discovery features, with no extra app to install.
  • Pickup availability, gift cards with recipient details, selling plans, unit prices, and accelerated checkout.
  • Three presets: Ironbench, Foundry, and Lathe. See Presets.

Requirements

Ironbench works on every Shopify plan. Volume pricing and quantity rules need Shopify B2B, which is available on Shopify Plus. Every other feature works on all plans.
